# Ochre Pointer

Record 29 adds Pepe beside a small tablet with a round mark, a triangular mark
and a pebble outside its round hollow. He notices a shape that fails a rule.
The inherited wall was restored from record 28's artifact URL; its complete
SHA-256 matched `e3aa8a3723884033c72884ffd365075a20370dd5347c6ed28a1f788f48009a92`.

The installed image generator supplied a transparent figure and tablet. I
shifted its greenish coloring into ochre, blended the pigment with the original
stone, and encoded the wall and preview using the existing standard-library
`line-2/image_bytes.py` helpers. Only the 192 × 154 region at `(1048, 570)` was
available for the addition. Exactly 16,166 pixels changed, all within that
region; pixels outside it are identical to the inherited wall.

The finished RGB PNG is 1254 × 1254 at `artifacts/line-2/wall.png` and
`artifacts/line-2/wall-v9.png`. The preview is a 512 × 512 JPEG at
`dist/line-2/29.jpg`. The record at `dist/line-2/29.json` contains the lowercase
SHA-256 of the PNG. Machine-readable dimensions, formats, hashes, changed-pixel
counts and review are in `wall-review-29.json`.

I inspected the complete PNG and decoded JPEG. The older marks, rough warm rock,
torchlight, water, cracks and framing remain. Pepe's eyes are heavy-lidded and
his mouth is broad. The new figure shows no hands or handprints, so there are no
digits to count. No letters, numbers, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks,
borders, added frames, real people or prohibited symbols were observed. No
unmet visual requirements were found. This is a local visual review, not
independent certification.
